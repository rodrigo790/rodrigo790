## About me

I am a Biological Engineering student at **Beijing University of Chemical Technology (BUCT)**. My work and learning sit at the intersection of computational biology, reproducible research, infectious disease, and global scientific collaboration.

My journey has taken me from El Salvador to China and Barcelona. I care about building practical links between Latin America, China, and the international scientific community—and about making research transparent enough for others to inspect, reproduce, and improve.

## What I am building

- 🧬 **Computational immunoinformatics** for transparent, in-silico prioritization of infectious-disease research hypotheses.
- 🧪 **Structural bioinformatics** to compare predicted and experimental protein structures.
- 🦟 **Public-health data tools** for reproducible dengue surveillance and forecasting.
- 🌍 **Science across borders**, informed by experiences with BUCT, ERASMUS MUNDUS LIVE, iGEM, and international outreach.

## Featured projects

| Project | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [**DENV-2 epitope prioritization**](https://github.com/rodrigo790/denv2-epitope-prioritization) | An auditable, in-silico workflow that prioritizes DENV-2 envelope MHC-I candidate peptides for further research. It clearly distinguishes computational hypotheses from experimental validation. |
| [**Dengue Surveillance Monitor**](https://github.com/rodrigo790/dengue-surveillance-monitor) | A reproducible dashboard for aggregate dengue surveillance in Central America, Mexico, and Puerto Rico, with a historical one-week-ahead machine-learning benchmark. |
| [**Yeast Structure Benchmark**](https://github.com/rodrigo790/yeast-structure-benchmark) | An educational structural-bioinformatics comparison of a yeast PGK1 AlphaFold prediction and the experimental 3PGK structure. |

## Reproducible by design

Across my public projects, I aim to make the scientific path as useful as the final result:

- versioned data snapshots and recorded provenance;
- locked dependencies and containerized workflows;
- automated checks that can run without network access; and
- explicit boundaries around what computational analyses can—and cannot—claim.

## Beyond the lab

I enjoy sharing the human side of an international scientific journey: learning across languages and cultures, taking part in scientific communities, and staying curious about how biotechnology can address global challenges.
